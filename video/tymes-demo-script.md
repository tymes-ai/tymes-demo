# Tymes demo: video script

The script for `tymes-demo.mp4`, the narrated walkthrough of this demo. One founder, Lena, runs two campaigns from one Tymes account. The video is not in this repository.

- **Length:** 4:27, at 1920 × 1080
- **Narration:** 710 words, one narrator
- **Captions:** a strip above the timeline bar, labeled by campaign
- **Say "Tymes" as "times".** Threeday is "three-day".

Times are when each line starts. Read the narration at a natural pace, and leave a short pause between sections. "..." marks a deliberate pause.

## Opening

### 1. 0:00

**Screen:** Title card: "One founder. Two campaigns."

> So, here's the thing about being an early-stage founder. You need more of the right conversations. Customers to learn from, investors to pitch... and you never have the time to go and get them. This is Lena, who's building a startup called Threeday. Let's see what happens when Tymes takes that job on.

## Setup

### 2. 0:19

**Screen:** First page, then the login screen. Lena continues with Google.

**Caption:** Setting up: Lena signs up once. This is the only time Tymes asks for all of this.

> Getting started is just one login. And honestly, this is the only time Tymes asks for any of this.

### 3. 0:26

**Screen:** Payment step. The four packs: 10, 20, 50, 100 meetings, each with its target count. Lena picks 10.

**Caption:** Meeting packs: Lena buys meetings up front, in packs of 10, 20, 50, or 100. Each pack sets the list: 10 targets per meeting.

> Now, here's the model, and it's simple. You don't pay per user, and you're not really buying software. You're buying meetings, up front, in packs of ten, twenty, fifty, or a hundred.

### 4. 0:38

**Screen:** Payment step, "What you pay for": meetings you had use 1, everything else uses 0. Lena buys 10 meetings.

**Caption:** Meeting packs: Lena pays only for meetings that actually happen. Outreach and missed meetings use nothing.

> And a meeting only comes out of your pack when it actually happens. So Tymes gets paid in advance... but it only earns that money by delivering.

### 5. 0:46

**Screen:** Company step. Tymes reads the website and drafts the profile. Lena confirms it.

**Caption:** Setting up: Tymes reads the website and drafts a company profile.

> Give it a website, and it reads the company and drafts a full profile. Lena gives it a quick look, and moves on.

### 6. 0:54

**Screen:** Accounts step. Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Meet, and LinkedIn connect.

**Caption:** Setting up: The mailbox and calendar connect once, for every campaign.

> The mailbox and the calendar connect once, and that's it. Everything goes out from the founder's own email.
