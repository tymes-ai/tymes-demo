Privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy covers the Tymes demo at demo.tymes.ai. Tymes ("Tymes", "we") is not yet incorporated, so the team behind it is responsible for the personal data described here. To reach us, email samarth.kapatel5@gmail.com.

The demo is a simulation. It sends no real outreach, takes no real payments, and does not connect to real email, calendar, or LinkedIn accounts.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your name, email address, and profile photo, and the account ID from Google, Microsoft, or LinkedIn if you sign in with one of them. If you sign up with email, the address and any password you set. Clerk stores passwords; we cannot read them.
  • Sign-in activity. When you signed up and last signed in, and technical details such as your IP address, browser, and device.
  • Security and hosting data. Sign-in forms may run an automated bot check, and our host keeps standard request logs.

We do not ask Google, Microsoft, or LinkedIn for your inbox, calendar, contacts, or connections.

What stays in your browser

Your progress through the demo, and anything you type into it, is saved in your browser's local storage. It is not sent to us. Clearing your site data removes it.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep the demo secure.
  • To know who has viewed the demo and when.
  • To follow up with you about Tymes. You can ask us to stop at any time.

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on our legitimate interest in running and securing the demo and in talking with people interested in Tymes.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers on the demo. We do not use information from your Google account for any purpose other than those listed here.

Who handles it for us

  • Clerk provides sign-in and stores account details.
  • Vercel hosts the demo.
  • Cloudflare provides DNS and the bot check.
  • Google, Microsoft, and LinkedIn confirm your identity if you sign in with them. Their own privacy policies apply to your accounts with them.

These companies may process data in the United States and other countries. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

Cookies

Sign-in uses cookies that keep you signed in and protect your session. The demo needs them to work. We do not use advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

We delete account details within 12 months of your last sign-in, or sooner if you ask. Our providers may keep logs and backups for a limited time under their own policies.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your data, or to stop using it to contact you, by emailing samarth.kapatel5@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, including objecting to how we use your data and complaining to your data protection authority.

Children

The demo is meant for business use and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
